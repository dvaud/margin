# Choosing the shared folder

> **In short**
> - On the *Team* page, a dialog lists the OneDrive, Google Drive, Box and Dropbox folders of your computer by name:
>   press **Use** beside the one that holds your team's folder.
> - **Choose folder…** opens the system's own folder window; pasting the whole path still works.
> - A colleague's Margin finds the team's folder among their cloud folders and offers it: they confirm, not search.
> - A folder shared with you has to be made available on your computer first (below).

## The one who starts

1. Open *Team*, under the project's name. In a project without a team, under *Start a team for this project*, pick *A folder you
   already share* and press *Choose the folder…*. (A colleague who only joins presses *Choose the shared folder…* under
   *Join a colleague's team*, which opens the same dialog under the title *Join through a folder your colleague
   shared*.) A dialog opens, *Start with a folder you share*.
2. In the dialog, choose the folder your team shares:
   - **Cloud folders on this computer** lists the OneDrive, Google Drive, Box and Dropbox folders this computer has
     (on a Mac those in `~/Library/CloudStorage/` and `~/Dropbox`; on Windows those OneDrive and Dropbox name).
     Press **Use** beside one; a folder inside it is chosen with the next way.
   - **Choose folder…** opens the system's own folder window. It is not shown on a computer that has none.
   - Or paste the whole path: `/Users/anna/Dropbox/Our project` on a Mac, `C:\Users\Anna\OneDrive\Our project` on
     Windows. To copy a folder's path: in the Finder, select it and press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>C</kbd>; in File
     Explorer, hold <kbd>Shift</kbd>, right-click it and choose *Copy as path*.
3. Whichever way, the path goes in the field *The shared folder* and nothing happens until you press **Use this folder**
   ([the switch for documents kept out](together.md#documents-kept-out-of-the-folder) is decided there too).

## A colleague joins

Send them what you shared, as a file (*Send it once, as a file* on the Team page); they open it with *Import a project*
under *Projects*. In their copy, the Team page looks for your team's folder (`Margin-` and a few letters) among the
cloud folders of their computer, a few folders deep and for about two seconds, and shows a notice at the top, *Your
colleague's shared folder is on this computer*: they press **Use this folder…**, then **Use this folder** in the
dialog. Until then, your Team page names the folder as you have it (under *The shared folder* on *People and
settings*), for example *OneDrive – University of Amsterdam › Our project*, so they know what to look
for. Its path on their computer may differ.

## When the folder is not on their computer yet

In the dialog this is the small fold *Your colleague's folder is not here yet?*

A folder shared with you has to be made available on your computer before Margin can use it.

- **OneDrive.** On OneDrive's website, open the shared folder and choose *Add shortcut to My files*. In the OneDrive
  app, choose *Always keep on this device* for it, so its files are there and not fetched only when opened.
- **Google Drive and Dropbox.** Accept the share and keep the folder available offline.

It then shows in the dialog's list. If Margin finds nothing, choose the folder by hand.

Margin makes a folder of its own in the shared folder for the project; leave what is inside as it is. It refuses a
folder that holds the project itself or Margin's own folder, and says why under the field. What the folder holds, and
who can read it: [Privacy](privacy.md#working-together).
